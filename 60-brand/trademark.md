# Trademark and marks

**Status:** Accepted

## The position

The **code** is source-available under the Hippocratic License 3.0
([ADR-0003](../00-overview/decisions/0003-hippocratic-3-0-license.md)). The
**name and marks** are a separate matter, and the licence does not grant rights
to them.

This is the standard arrangement for a source-available project and it exists for
one reason: a user must be able to tell whether the thing they downloaded is
Beatrax.

## What you may do

- **Use the name to refer to the project.** Write about it, review it, compare
  it, criticise it.
- **State that your work is based on Beatrax**, or compatible with it, or a fork
  of it — accurately.
- **Reproduce the logo** in documentation, articles, and talks, unmodified, at a
  size where it reads.
- **Fork the code** and distribute your fork, under the licence's terms.

## What you may not do

- **Distribute a modified build under the Beatrax name**
  ([DES-R11](README.md#the-des-r-namespace)). Change the name.
- **Use the name or marks in a way that implies endorsement**, affiliation, or
  official status.
- **Register a confusingly similar name or mark**, or a domain that would be
  taken for an official one.
- **Alter the logo** — recolour, distort, recompose, or use it as an element in
  another mark.

## Why a fork must rename

The product's entire proposition is a promise about what it does with the user's
financial data. A modified build carrying the name inherits that promise without
being bound by it.

A user who downloads "Beatrax" from somewhere and finds it phoning home has been
harmed by the name, not by the code. Renaming is what makes that
distinguishable.

**This is not a restriction on forking.** Fork freely; the licence permits it.
Just do not call it Beatrax.

## The publisher

NightWorks.io. The publisher name and its mark are likewise not granted by the
code licence.

## Screenshots and assets

Screenshots of the application may be reproduced for the purposes above.
Screenshots used **publicly by the project** come from the product repository and
show a version that exists ([DES-R12](README.md#the-des-r-namespace)).

Brand assets — the logo files, the icons — live with the product and are not
granted for arbitrary reuse.

## Open question

**Nothing is formally registered.** There is no registered trademark, and this
page states a position rather than asserting a registration.

Whether registration is worth pursuing is undecided. The position above is what
the project asks for and what it will say if asked; enforcement beyond that is
not currently a thing this project is set up to do.
